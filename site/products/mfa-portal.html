Self-service multi-factor enrollment and management, backed by the directory you already run. Users register, replace and recover their own second factors instead of opening a ticket for every reset.

What it will do

Self-service enrollment

Users register their own second factors, and replace a lost one, without the help desk standing in the middle of it.

Directory-backed

Identities and group membership come from LDAP, so enrollment policy follows the structure you have already defined.

Administrative oversight

Administrators can see who is enrolled, who is not, and act on the accounts that still need a factor.
